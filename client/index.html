Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about using SmartBook Pro as a customer or business owner.

SmartBook Pro is a multi-business service booking platform designed to connect customers with local service providers. Customers can discover businesses, browse services, check real-time availability, and book appointments online. Business owners can manage their schedules, services, staff, and customer bookings in a centralized system.

Booking an appointment is straightforward: browse or search for a business by category or location, select the specific service and practitioner you need, choose an available date and time slot from the live calendar, and confirm your reservation.

Yes. Customers can view their active appointments inside their account portal or via their booking confirmation link and request a reschedule or cancellation in accordance with the individual business's cancellation policies.

Yes. Service providers such as barbers, salons, wellness spas, clinics, fitness trainers, and home technicians can register their business on SmartBook Pro. Once registered, businesses will have dedicated dashboard tools to set up services, staff schedules, and manage incoming customer appointments.

Appointment availability is generated in real time based on each business's operational hours, staff shift schedules, and currently confirmed bookings. As soon as a slot is booked, it is instantly updated to prevent double bookings.

Yes. Once an appointment is booked, SmartBook Pro sends an automated email confirmation containing appointment details, service duration, location information, and calendar integration links. Automated reminder notifications are also scheduled prior to the visit.

For customers, searching and booking appointments on SmartBook Pro is free. For business owners, flexible onboarding tiers and management plans will be available as backend registration and administration modules roll out.
